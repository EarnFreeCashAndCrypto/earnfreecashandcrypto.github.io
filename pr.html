Your Privacy Matters

Last updated: January 2026

At EFCAC ("we", "us", "our"), your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information We Collect

We keep data collection to the absolute minimum. We do not require registration, and we do not ask for personal information to browse our site. The only data we may collect:

  • Anonymous analytics — Aggregated data about page visits, referrers, device type, and country. This data cannot identify you personally.
  • Cookies — Small text files used to remember your preferences and measure site performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.
  • Voluntary contact — If you email us, we receive your email address and message. We use it only to respond to you.

2. How We Use Your Information

The limited data we collect is used to:

  • Improve the content and usability of the site
  • Understand which earning platforms are most helpful to visitors
  • Respond to your emails, feedback, or support requests
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulations

We never sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Links

EFCAC links to external earning platforms, faucets, and apps. Once you click an external link, you leave our site and are subject to that platform's own privacy policy. We strongly recommend reading the privacy policy of any site before signing up or sharing personal information.

4. Cookies

We use a small number of cookies for analytics and site functionality. These cookies do not collect personally identifiable information. You can disable cookies entirely through your browser preferences — this will not affect your ability to use EFCAC.

5. Analytics

We use privacy-friendly analytics to understand visitor behavior in aggregate. No personal data is stored, and IP addresses are anonymized. We do not use invasive tracking scripts or cross-site advertising networks.

6. Data Retention

Anonymous analytics data is retained for up to 12 months. Emails you send us are kept as long as necessary to handle your request, then deleted.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your country of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to any personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to processing of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at the address below.

8. Children's Privacy

EFCAC is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided us data, please contact us immediately so we can remove it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. When we do, we'll revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of EFCAC after changes means you accept the updated policy.
